## Epic

### Airport Lost Luggage Recovery Lifecycle

Enable passengers and baggage service agents to report, track, investigate, recover, return, and compensate lost-luggage claims through one traceable workflow.

| Key | Rank | Story | User story | Priority | Story points | Status |
|---|---:|---|---|---|---:|---|

| SCRUM-2 | 2 | Match Claim with Baggage Scan Logs | As a Baggage Service Agent, I want the portal to cross-reference a claim with unrouted baggage scan logs, so that a likely bag location can be identified quickly. | High | 5 | Done |

| SCRUM-4 | 4 | Investigate Claim and Arrange Return | As a Baggage Service Agent, I want to add investigation notes, update claim status, associate a recovered bag, and record delivery details, so that recovery actions remain controlled and traceable. | High | 5 | Done |
